01 // about

  
Firmware that talks to hardware,
and people who depend on it.

  

    

      
I'm an embedded firmware engineer who enjoys the full stack of a device: reading the schematic, bringing up the board, writing the drivers, and building the user interface on top.

      
At Clarity Medical I work on monitoring systems for ECG, SpO2, heart rate, respiration and ASSR (Auditory Steady State Response), including a custom GUI framework written from scratch for a TFT display.

      
Before that, at Advance Tech India, I designed IoT gateways and dataloggers for agriculture and industry, with GSM/WiFi/Ethernet connectivity, Modbus, FreeRTOS and custom FOTA updates.
